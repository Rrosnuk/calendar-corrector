Calendar Corrector

Your schedule, the way you want it.

Calendar Corrector fixes the calendars you subscribe to — work shifts, school, sports and holidays — and keeps a clean copy in your own calendar. Your originals are never changed.

  • Shifts that end after midnight stay on the right day
  • Your job name first in every title, so two jobs never blur together
  • Only the holidays that apply where you live
  • Alerts, birthday reminders and colours for any kind of event
  • Fixes apply automatically to every future event
  • If a feed breaks, you keep the last good version and get a warning

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. Available in 50 languages.
