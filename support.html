Support

Answers to the common questions. Still stuck? Email lugnutservice@protonmail.com and include your device and the calendar you're fixing.

I see every event twice

Your original calendar and the corrected copy are both showing. Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars and uncheck the original. It keeps syncing in the background, and Calendar Corrector still reads from it. Apps aren't allowed to hide calendars, so this one step is yours.

Where do I find my calendar link?

  • Scheduling apps (7shifts, Deputy, When I Work, Homebase): look for “Sync to calendar” or “Calendar feed” and copy the link.
  • Google Calendar: on a computer, open the calendar's Settings and sharing › Integrate calendar, and copy “Secret address in iCal format”.
  • Already on your iPhone or Mac? You don't need a link. Choose it from the list of calendars on your device.

My calendar stopped updating

The app keeps showing the last good version and warns you instead of emptying your calendar. Usually the link changed: get a new one from where it came from, then use Replace link in the calendar's settings.

A shift spills into the next day

Some schedules leave out the end time. When you add the calendar, the app offers to fix this. You can also tap the event and change its end time for all similar events.

I deleted an event in Calendar and it came back

That's on purpose, so nothing from your schedule gets lost. To remove an event for good, open it in Calendar Corrector and turn on Hide.

Subscriptions

Your first calendar is free. Unlimited is managed by Apple: cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, and use Restore purchases in the app on a new device.
