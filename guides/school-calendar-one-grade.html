Only show your child's grade in a school calendar subscription

The school's calendar has every grade's field trips, band practices and forms, and you only care about one.

Why the school calendar is so busy

Most schools publish one calendar for the whole school. It's simpler for them, but it means a Grade 3 parent sees the Grade 7 ski trip, the Grade 5 band practice and every other class's deadline. You can't delete them, because a subscribed calendar is read-only.

Hide every other grade in two taps per grade

  1. Add the school calendar to Calendar Corrector.
  2. Tap any event for a grade you don't need, such as "Grade 5 · Band Practice".
  3. Turn on Hide this event, tap Save, and choose All events with "Grade 5".
  4. Repeat for each other grade.

School-wide events like PA days, holidays and concerts stay. New events for the hidden grades are hidden automatically as the school adds them.

More fixes for school calendars

  • If every title starts with the school's name, remove it in one tap.
  • Show PA days and holidays as busy so nobody books you over them.
  • Add an alert the evening before field trips and form deadlines.
