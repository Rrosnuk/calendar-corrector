  • How to edit a subscribed calendar on iPhone, iPad or Mac
    Apple Calendar won't let you edit events in a calendar you subscribe to. Here's why, and how to fix titles, times and more without breaking the subscription.
  • Work shifts ending at 6 AM? Fix overnight shift times in your calendar
    Scheduling apps often publish closing shifts with a placeholder end time, so a 5 PM shift shows as ending at 6 AM the next day. Fix every one automatically.
  • Only show your child's grade in a school calendar subscription
    School calendars list events for every grade. Hide the field trips, practices and deadlines for other grades, and keep school-wide days like PA days and holidays.
  • Change event titles in a calendar you subscribe to
    Turn cryptic titles like 'SVR-2 PM FOH' into something readable, and keep the change on every future event, even though the calendar is read-only.
  • Hide holidays that don't apply where you live
    Country holiday calendars include every province and state. Keep only the days off where you live, in your language, and mark them busy.
  • Make all-day events show as busy instead of free
    All-day events from subscribed calendars count as free time, so people can book over your days off. Mark them busy automatically.
  • Sports team calendar: arrive early and colour away games
    Team calendars show game time, not when you need to be there. Start events 30 minutes early, add the arena address, and give away games their own colour.
  • Subscribed calendar stopped updating or went blank?
    When a calendar feed breaks or comes back empty, your iPhone may show an empty schedule. Keep the last good version and get warned instead.
